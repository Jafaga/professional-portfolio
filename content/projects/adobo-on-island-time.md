I made Adobo on Island Time to bring together two things I love: making chicken adobo and building with code. It was not an assignment or a deadline. I wanted to document my own recipe and the small family details around it in a way that felt personal and easy to explore.

The site presents six cooking moments through an animated, responsive timeline. Each step includes directions, ingredients, timing, cooking cues, personal notes, and food-safety guidance. Building it showed me that a technical project can also carry culture, memory, and identity.
