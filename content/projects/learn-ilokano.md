Learn Ilokano is the project that connects my computer science work with my background in Philippine Language and Culture. The current prototype has 10 learning units, 22 lesson milestones, and 220 questions. Learners practice short beginner lessons, master a milestone to unlock the next one, and keep XP and streak progress in their browser.

The current wording reflects my understanding and experience. Before the project grows, I want fluent speakers, educators, and community contributors to review and improve the language content.
