01 / OVERVIEW

The project at a glance

Every eighth-grade team selected a real-world issue, researched it over several months, and presented a responsible explanation to a public audience.

THE TOPIC

Homelessness in the local community

My group chose an issue we had noticed around us and wanted to understand beyond our first impressions.

THE WORK

Research, organize, and explain

We studied causes, community effects, local statistics, shelters, aid programs, and the resources available to people.

THE DELIVERY

A professional team presentation

We turned the research into a clear slide narrative and presented it to teachers, classmates, community members, and a review panel.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

My responsibilities within the team

I helped move the project from raw research to a presentation the audience could follow.

RESEARCH

Causes and local resources

I focused on why homelessness happens and what support was available through local shelters and aid programs.

INFORMATION DESIGN

Professional slide structure

I helped design the PowerPoint, decide how much information each slide needed, and make the visual flow feel consistent.

TEAM COORDINATION

Speaker order and transitions

Our group organized who would explain each section and practiced the handoffs until the full presentation felt connected.

PRESENTATION

Opening the final delivery

I was the first speaker. Setting the stage was nerve-racking, but it strengthened my confidence in front of a public audience.

03 / PROCESS

A months-long research workflow

  1. 01SelectChoose a visible community issue worth examining carefully.
  2. 02InvestigateCompare information, local statistics, causes, effects, and available resources.
  3. 03SynthesizeTurn many findings into a focused slide narrative with clear speaking roles.
  4. 04PresentPractice repeatedly, then deliver the work to a public review audience.
04 / OUTCOMES

Skills that still carry into my work

RESEARCH

Finding and organizing reliable information

I learned how to compare sources, identify the points that mattered, and explain them without overwhelming the audience.

COMMUNICATION

Presenting a difficult issue responsibly

The project strengthened my teamwork, long-term planning, slide design, public speaking, and confidence.

WHY IT MATTERS

The final presentation was only one part of the result. The months of questions, revisions, practice, and collaboration are what made the project meaningful.
